# Ad copy for /start

Ready-to-paste copy for every ad-matched version of `/start`. Each ad links to `/start?for=<key>`, and the page opens on the headline and example problem for that key (`AD_PAGES` in `outlines.js`). The leads list also counts by that key, so each ad must link to its own page.

`tests/ads.spec.mjs` checks this file against `outlines.js`: every key here has a page and every page has a section here, each link points to its own page, every line fits its platform's character limit, and the rules below that a script can check still hold.

## Before you run these

- The ads offer a free project outline and a free 15-minute call. Run them only once `/start` books calls for real. While the page still shows its "Prototype" notes, nobody can book a call from it.
- Edit the copy freely, but keep to the rules below and keep each section's layout, so the test can still read it.

## Rules the copy follows

- Say only what's true today: describe the problem in a sentence and get a project outline back on the page, no email needed to see it, a free 15-minute call, and Thomas plans and builds every project himself.
- No guarantees, numbers, statistics or percentages (the 15-minute call is the one exception), no "best" or "#1", no urgency or scarcity, no competitor names and no prices. Nothing about results or past work beyond what the Work section of the home page shows.
- Google: no words in all caps (AI is an acronym, which is allowed), no exclamation marks in headlines, no repeated punctuation and no phone numbers.
- Meta: ask about the business ("Missing calls while you're with a customer?"). Never state something about the person reading ("Your business is failing").

## How to use each section

- **Google Search:** one responsive search ad per page, each in its own ad group. Paste the final URL, all eight headlines and all three descriptions. Headlines are at most 30 characters and descriptions at most 90.
- **Meta (Facebook and Instagram):** one ad per page. Paste the primary text (at most 125 characters), the headline (at most 40) and the website URL.

## Anywhere else

Every other link follows the same pattern, with the page's key and the place the link appears:

```text
https://wright-ai-solutions.com/start?for=<key>&utm_source=<platform>
```

- `<key>` is one of the pages above: `leads`, `spreadsheets`, `support`, `app`, `missed-calls`, `reports`, `website` or `booking`. Pick the one that matches what the flyer, post or listing talks about.
- `<platform>` is where the link appears, in lowercase: `google`, `bing`, `meta`, `facebook`, `instagram`, `linkedin`, `tiktok`, `x`, `reddit`, `youtube`, `nextdoor`, `yelp`, `email` or `qr`. Anything else is counted as "other", and a link with no `utm_source` as "direct".

For example:

- **A flyer or door hanger:** make a QR code of `https://wright-ai-solutions.com/start?for=missed-calls&utm_source=qr`
- **An email signature:** `https://wright-ai-solutions.com/start?for=leads&utm_source=email`, or whichever page fits the people you email most.
- **Your own Facebook page or Instagram profile:** use `facebook` or `instagram`, and keep `meta` for paid ads, so unpaid posts and paid ads can be told apart.
- **Microsoft Advertising:** if you import the Google ads, change `utm_source=google` to `utm_source=bing` in each final URL, or those visits are counted as Google.

## Where the results show up

The leads list at `/admin` shows visits, outlines, saved and booked for each ad page and each platform. It counts by the link's `for` (the ad page) and `utm_source` (the platform), so give every link both.
