Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 23 September 2026

This notice covers wright-ai-solutions.com, run by Wright AI Solutions LLC (Thomas Wright), and what happens when you email or call us from it. Questions or requests go to t@thomasewright.com.

What this site collects

Nothing, directly. There are no forms, accounts, cookies, local storage, analytics or advertising trackers on this site. Its security policy only lets scripts load from this site itself, so third-party trackers can't run here. The site's one script opens the mobile menu, shows the current year, animates sections as you scroll, and copies our email or phone number to your clipboard when you click them; none of that leaves your browser.

What our host logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the technical details needed to deliver pages and block abuse, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and when. We don't use that data to identify or profile visitors. Cloudflare's own privacy policy, at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy, covers how it handles that data.

If you email or call us

We receive what you send: usually your name, email address or phone number, and your message. We use it only to reply to you and, if we end up working together, to run that project. We don't sell it or share it for advertising.

If an inquiry doesn't turn into a project, we delete it within two years of our last contact. If it does, we keep project records for as long as the contract and tax record-keeping rules require.

People and businesses in our work examples

The Work section describes projects we've built. Client names and figures in the AI Lead Response Agent example are sample data, not real results. If you're shown in, or can be identified from, anything on this site and want it changed or removed, email us and we'll take it down.

Your choices

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing t@thomasewright.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

Other sites

The Work section links to other sites we've built. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and this notice doesn't cover them.

Children

This site is for businesses and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this notice changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
