AI Lead Response Agent

An SMS agent that follows up with inbound leads for a subscription service business, integrated directly with their CRM (GoHighLevel).

It drafts contextual replies, detects when a message actually failed to deliver and recovers gracefully instead of repeating itself, and backs off for good once a person from the business joins the conversation. It runs on a schedule without anyone watching it. The client isn't named, and the dashboard shown here is a mockup filled with sample data, not the client's real results.

Automation

Automation

Data→Lead→Sell

An automated lead-generation platform for real estate investors, built and operated in-house. Every lead starts as a real obituary, cross-referenced against that county's property records until a decedent and a property owner actually match.

Owner mailing addresses get validated for deliverability, matched leads land on a live map dashboard, and a built-in PDF wizard turns them straight into mail-ready postcard templates — a pipeline that used to take hours of manual county-site digging, running unattended.
